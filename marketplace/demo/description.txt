Audio Switcher Plus (Demo) changes your Windows audio device from a Stream Deck key. It's the demo of Audio Switcher Plus: it switches between two outputs and two inputs of your choice. Every key shows which device is active.

Features
• Toggle Audio Device: one key flips between your two devices
• Set Audio Device: one key per device, lit while that device is active
• Output or input: switch speakers and headphones, or microphones
• Role: Default, or Communications for calling apps
• Live status: keys update instantly, even when you switch in Windows
• Follows your devices: finds a device again after Windows renumbers it
• Colors: On and Off colors for each key's icon
• Nothing extra to install: runs inside Stream Deck, so Windows Smart App Control has nothing to block

Setup
1. Drag "Toggle Audio Device" or "Set Audio Device" onto a key.
2. Choose Output or Input, then pick your device (two for Toggle). Pick another any time; the old one frees up once no key uses it.

Demo limits: two outputs and two inputs, and one icon each way (speaker, mic). The full version adds every device, the All role, and headphones and earbuds icons, and installs as a separate plugin:

Requirements: Windows 10 or later and Stream Deck 7.1 or later. Not available for macOS.

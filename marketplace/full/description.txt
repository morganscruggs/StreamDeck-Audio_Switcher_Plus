Audio Switcher Plus changes your Windows audio device from a Stream Deck key. Flip between headphones and speakers with one press, or give each device its own key. Every key shows which device is active. Works for microphones too.

Features
• Toggle Audio Device: one key flips between two devices (with Both, each brings its own mic)
• Set Audio Device: one key per device, lit while that device is active
• Output, input or both: e.g. a headset's headphones and mic together
• Role: Default, Communications (calling apps), or All for both
• Live status: keys update instantly, even when you switch in Windows
• Follows your devices: finds a device again after Windows renumbers it
• Clean device list: hide unplugged devices and device types
• Your icons and colors: headphones, speaker, earbuds or mic, in any color
• Sound settings keys: open the classic Sound control panel or Sound in Windows Settings
• Nothing to install: runs inside Stream Deck, so Smart App Control can't block it

Setup
1. Drag "Toggle Audio Device" or "Set Audio Device" onto a key.
2. Pick Output, Input or Both, then your device (two for Toggle).
3. Optionally pick an icon and colors.

Based on Fred Emmott's open-source Audio Switcher, with added features.

Requirements: Windows 10+ and Stream Deck 7.1+. Not available for macOS.
